**Huang Lab, University of Toronto** — Nov. 2025 – May 2026 (remote)
Supervisor: Prof. Xi Huang

Investigating the function and expression of human-specific genomic elements in medulloblastoma using public datasets including RNA-seq and ATAC-seq. Screened a series of candidate genes encoded by — or regulated by — these elements for further functional studies.

**Fu & Ptáček Lab, University of California, San Francisco** — Mar. 2025 – Jun. 2025
Supervisors: Prof. Ying-Hui Fu & Louis J. Ptáček

Worked on a sleep neuroscience project, conducting 3D visualization and quantitative analysis of neuronal projections. Contributed most of the code for a 3D reconstruction and cell-prediction tool based on continuous coronal mouse brain sections, now part of a manuscript in preparation. Also gained hands-on training in EEG and mouse stereotaxic surgery.

**Jin Lab, The First Hospital of Lanzhou University** — Nov. 2022 – Jan. 2025
Supervisor: Prof. Weilin Jin

Led a series of bioelectricity projects, including stimulation-parameter research and the establishment of a bioelectricity-related database. Designed and optimized electronic/magnetic stimulation systems for cellular and molecular biology experiments, and built a magnetic stimulation system with Helmholtz coils based on 3D printing, a signal generator, and a power amplifier. PC12 and N2a cell lines showed potentiated differentiation in response to 50 Hz sinusoidal and pulsed strong magnetic fields. This work was awarded the Outstanding Project of the Action Plan for Innovation and Entrepreneurship at Lanzhou University (1st in 51 projects, 2024), and contributed to a first-author review in *Trends in Molecular Medicine*.
