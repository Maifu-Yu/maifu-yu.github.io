This first-author review, written during my early undergraduate training in the Jin Lab, synthesizes how bioelectronic medicine can potentiate endogenous neural stem cells (NSCs) as a therapeutic strategy for neurodegenerative diseases. Writing it gave me a solid foundation in the frontiers of bioelectricity and neuroscience, and it motivated the stimulation-system projects I later led in the lab.
